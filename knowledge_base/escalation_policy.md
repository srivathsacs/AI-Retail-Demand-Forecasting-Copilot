# Escalation Policy

## Purpose

This document defines escalation requirements based on inventory risk levels and business impact metrics.

---

# LOW Risk

## Conditions

* Stockout Risk = LOW
* Overstock Risk = LOW
* Inventory Health Score >= 70

## Escalation Requirement

No escalation required.

## Responsible Role

Inventory Planner

## Required Action

Continue routine monitoring.

---

# MEDIUM Risk

## Conditions

* Stockout Risk = MEDIUM
* Overstock Risk = MEDIUM
* Inventory Health Score between 50 and 69

## Escalation Requirement

Planner review required.

## Responsible Role

Inventory Planner

## Required Action

* Review inventory position
* Review forecast updates
* Monitor inventory trends

## Response Timeline

Within 7 business days

---

# HIGH Risk

## Conditions

* Stockout Risk = HIGH
* Overstock Risk = HIGH
* Revenue Risk > 0
* Potential Lost Sales > 0

## Escalation Requirement

Management notification required.

## Responsible Role

Inventory Planner
Category Manager

## Required Action

* Review inventory exposure
* Evaluate replenishment options
* Document mitigation plan

## Response Timeline

Within 2 business days

---

# CRITICAL Risk

## Conditions

* Stockout Risk = CRITICAL
* Inventory Health Score < 30

## Escalation Requirement

Immediate management escalation required.

## Responsible Role

Inventory Planner
Category Manager
Retail Operations Manager

## Required Action

* Immediate inventory review
* Create mitigation plan
* Initiate replenishment review
* Monitor situation daily

## Response Timeline

Within 24 hours

---

# Escalation Principles

## Principle 1

Escalate inventory issues before inventory depletion occurs.

## Principle 2

Inventory Health Score should be reviewed together with risk classifications.

## Principle 3

Business impact metrics should be considered when prioritizing responses.

## Principle 4

Document all actions taken for HIGH and CRITICAL risk events.
